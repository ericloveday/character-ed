Character Ed Volunteer Manager

Sign in required

Sign in with your Cornerstone Google account to manage the Character Ed volunteer schedule.

1. Roster

Upload a CSV file with Last, First columns. A header row is fine — it's ignored automatically. Uploading a new roster replaces the current one.

2. Generate Wednesdays

Auto-generates every Wednesday between two dates. Existing dates (and their signups) are never overwritten — safe to re-run.

3. Schedule

Uncheck "School day" to mark off a break/holiday Wednesday — it will be hidden from the student sign-up form. Use Edit to manually assign or change a volunteer/friend.
